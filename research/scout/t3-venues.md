# T3 — Venue shortlist for a 2027 WBR paper

**Checked:** 30 October 2026. Deadlines below are published 2027 dates where available; otherwise they are the latest verified cycle and a planning cue, not a predicted deadline. “Acceptance rate unavailable” means I found no official rate, not that selectivity is low. Confirm the new call before submission.

## Paper shapes assumed

- **A — perception-to-advisory:** Pi 5 + depth camera terrain/obstacle estimate, uncertainty and bounded-latency advisory to an MCU. Strongest when the real WBR and safety integration are measured.
- **B — freshness contract:** Age-of-information/staleness supervisor and fault-injection evaluation on MCU/ROS 2 or micro-ROS. Could stand on processor-in-the-loop and deterministic timing evidence, with claims carefully bounded.
- **C — wheel-biped perception/control:** terrain-aware negotiation/jump behavior and evaluation against baselines. Needs the delivered robot, repeated trials and sound comparisons; current assumed-parameter simulation alone is insufficient for a robotics claim.

## Journals (continuous submission; no annual deadline)

- **Journal of Robotics and Mechatronics (JRM):** A reasonable applied robotics/mechatronics target for mature **A/C** with a useful system contribution and rigorous evidence. Author instructions describe novelty, usefulness and academic rigor review; regular research papers should normally be 6 printed pages, and authors are asked to survey the international literature and list at least 20 references. Publisher states first reviewer reports are requested within four weeks (not a guaranteed decision time). Open access with an article-processing charge. [Instructions for authors](https://www.fujipress.jp/main/wp-content/themes/Fujipress/JRM/pdf/jrm_ITA.pdf), [author page](https://www.fujipress.jp/jrm/rb-authors/). No acceptance rate found.
- **Robotics (MDPI):** Scope covers robot systems, autonomy, sensor fusion, learning, control software and applications; no fixed maximum length. Potential home for full **A/C** results, but budget the article-processing charge and use the journal’s actual author instructions for fees and peer-review details. The official pages were rate-limited when opened; details beyond scope/length are **UNVERIFIED** here. [Aims and scope](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/robotics/about). No acceptance rate included.
- **Sensors (MDPI):** Possible for a sensor/perception measurement paper (**A**) if the paper’s central contribution is sensing, calibration, uncertainty or validated terrain measurement, not merely using a camera on a robot. Check the live author instructions and fees. Official page was rate-limited during checking; length, review timing and fees are **UNVERIFIED** here. [Journal page](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/sensors).
- **IEEE Access:** Broad applied-engineering scope can fit **A/B** if the contribution is original, technically correct and backed by adequate experiments. The publisher says binary accept/reject with feedback, 4–6 weeks submission-to-publication, and about 20% average acceptance; these are journal-level statistics, not a promise for a particular paper. No page limit, but the journal recommends staying under 20 pages; APC applies. [About and metrics](https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/about/), [submission guidelines](https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/authors/submission-guidelines/). For a student-led paper, choose only if the study is sufficiently complete and the team can cover the APC.

## Practical ranking

1. **Regional first paper:** target the next RIVF or ATC call for a bounded, evidence-led **B** paper or a measured **A** system paper; select by actual 2027 scope and deadline, not habit.
2. **Robot-science paper:** once the arriving platform supports repeatable physical trials, consider IROS/ICRA for **C** or a journal for a longer integrated **A/C** study. ICRA 2027’s main submission window is already closed; a workshop is still a networking opportunity, with its own call.
3. **Avoid premature journal submission:** current tabletop perception and assumed-parameter simulation are useful preliminary evidence, but alone do not support broad terrain-negotiation, safety or real-robot claims. A new paper should state precisely which evidence is measured and which remains assumed.

No official acceptance rates were found for the listed conferences; I have not substituted guesses or third-party estimates. Publication and indexing claims should be rechecked in each new call. A conference and journal submission must not be under simultaneous review; if extending a conference paper, disclose and substantially distinguish the new contribution under the target publisher’s policy.
