# Breadboard drawing parts credits

The Raspberry Pi, Nucleo-64, GY-521 MPU6050, LED, and 220 Ω resistor drawings
were obtained from the `fritzing/fritzing-parts` repository and are provided
under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 license (CC-BY-SA 3.0).

Files used:

- `STM32_Nucleo_board_breadboard.svg` and `STM32_Nucleo_board.fzp`
- `raspberry-pi-4B_1_breadboard.svg` and `raspberry-pi-4B_1.fzp`
- `MPU6050_GY521_9.svg` and `InvenSense_MPU6050.fzp`
- `LED-5mm-red-leg.svg` and `LED-generic-5mm_6852162_005.fzp`
- `resistor_220.svg` and `resistor.fzp`

The Raspberry Pi drawing is a Pi 4B used to depict the Pi 5 because both use
the same 40-pin header layout. `sn65hvd230_module.svg` is our own simplified
module drawing, not an upstream Fritzing part.
