4.5 Worked window-rate and boundary check
KNOWN method / SYMBOLIC example
Let a requested window have duration T, N received frames, and first/last arrivals a1, aN relative to its
start.
Whole-window rate N/T measures coverage; (N− 1)/(aN− a1) measures only the surviving span. Both
are needed. Boundary silence is B= max(a1, T− aN); inspect adjacent arrival and source-stamp gaps
separately.
Burst delivery can have a nominal interior rate and long boundary silence.
