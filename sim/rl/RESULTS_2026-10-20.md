# First frozen PPO–LQR comparison — 2026-10-20

**MEASURED in an ASSUMED simulation:** delay-aware LQR succeeded in160/160
held-out episodes. Each of five PPO final policies succeeded in0/160 episodes
(0/800 total). The experiment completed normally; unsuccessful policies were
retained. This result concerns this plant, observation, reward and training
budget. It does not establish that RL cannot balance a WBR.

## What was held equal

Both controllers used the same reduced six-state MuJoCo plant,2ms action period,
2/3/4/5ms exact transport delays,5ms torque lag, body angle/gyro noise,2/4Nm
pair torque limits, initial state and6N×0.15s push at3s. Both received the same
measurements and causal command-history/torque estimates, with known delay;
truth was used only for reward/evaluation. No privileged critic or LQR residual.
See the [frozen protocol](protocol.json), [runtime](../augmented.md) and
[implementation guide](README.md).

The original gains first failed40/40 settling checks when imperfections were
added; conditional delay-state LQR then passed40/40, with Q/R unchanged. Its
four matrices were frozen before PPO training. The PPO comparison therefore
uses that named, frozen baseline, rather than selecting a controller per episode.

## What the interval means

The predeclared endpoint equally averages eight conditions within each training
seed. All five PPO-minus-LQR differences are−1; the seed-level mean is−1,
sample SD0 and95% Student-t interval `[−1,−1]` (df4). This degenerate interval
comes from identical binary success summaries in these five seeds. It does not
mean zero uncertainty about another noise panel, training budget, design or
real robot. There is no RL-superiority claim.

**MEASURED:** the frozen direct-torque PPO recipe did not solve the task within
this budget. **MEASURED in training:** only10/1379 started push episodes received any force
(nine complete pulses and one partial); two seeds received none. All2783 completed
training episodes physically terminated; five budget-ending partial episodes were
retained. **INFERRED, not established:** short episodes and little exposure
to the later push may limit learning. No ablation identifies the cause; reward,
optimization, exploration and sample budget have not been compared. No extra
training or protocol changes were made after seeing held-out outcomes.
