3.7 Payload budget
KNOWN profile / CALCULATED payload
Y8 stores one eight-bit grayscale byte per pixel; no compression assumed.
IR1 payload: 640 × 480 × 1 × 15 = 4.608 MB/s.
Stereo image payload doubles to 9.216 MB/s before overhead.
Metadata, motion, serialization and filesystem traffic add load.
Engineering decision
Qualify sustained recording under real load; theoretical USB speed is not available throughput.
Payload arithmetic is a lower bound, not throughput evidence.
