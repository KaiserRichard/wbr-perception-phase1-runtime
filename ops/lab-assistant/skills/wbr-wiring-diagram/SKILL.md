# Breadboard wiring diagrams

Output: `<name>.png/.pdf/.svg` from a YAML spec, rendered by
`tools/wiring_diagram/wiring_diagram.py`. Worked example (F446 bench, 30 wires):
`reports/2026-10-supervisor/figures/src/bench_wiring.yaml` -> `figures/breadboard-wiring.png`.

**Route every wire by hand with waypoints.** Six rounds of automatic routing
(free curves, A*, channel lanes) never converged: shared lanes, wires on the
wrong header, stubs. The tool has no router on purpose. You choose every corner;
the tool checks the geometry; you look at the PNG.

## 1. Pin truth first
- Take connections from the firmware/bench README pin table, never from an old picture
  (F446 bench: `tools/link_bench/README.md`, `tools/microros_f446/README.md`).
- Write the pin table (wire, from pin, to pin, colour) before drawing. Note anything not
  measured (e.g. "120 ohm on module, not measured") on the figure.
- Nucleo-64: Arduino headers CN5/CN6/CN8/CN9 sit inside the morpho rows CN7/CN10.
  A wire on D2 is a different hole from the same signal on CN10-33. Fritzing names
  D14/D15 `A4/SDA`, `A5/SCL` (Uno heritage); use connector ids in the spec.

## 2. Board drawings
- Source: `fritzing/fritzing-parts` on GitHub, `svg/core/breadboard/*.svg` plus the
  matching `core/*.fzp` (connector id -> breadboard `svgId`). Licence CC-BY-SA 3.0:
  credit in the figure and caption, share derived figures alike.
- Copy both files into `tools/wiring_diagram/parts/` and add a line to `PARTS_CREDITS.md`.
- No Fritzing part? Draw a small SVG with pin elements `id="pin_NAME"` and use
  `svg_pin_prefix: pin_` (see `sn65hvd230_module.svg`). Breadboard, resistors (bands
  from `ohms`) and LEDs are generated by the tool.
- Use one scale for all parts: pin pitch about 21.6 px (2.54 mm). Nucleo `scale: 3.0`,
  Pi 4B drawing `0.213`, GY-521 `0.216`, own module drawn at 20 px pitch -> `1.08`.

## 3. Layout and routing method
1. Place parts by hand. Leave 60-120 px corridors between boards for wire lanes and
   keep titles and notes out of corridors (they are obstacles).
2. `--print-pins PART REGEX` prints canvas x,y of every pin; `--grid` writes a draft PNG
   with a 50 px grid. Put the numbers in the spec, not in your head.
3. Each wire: `via: [y=..., x=..., y=...]` (axis moves) or `[x, y]` corners.
   - Leave a header perpendicular to its row; jog at most one half pitch between rows
     when a pin of another wire sits in the way (e.g. D4 under CN10-30).
   - One lane per wire, at least 10-15 px apart; nested L/U shapes keep order:
     the leftmost pin takes the outermost lane and the farthest destination.
   - Power: red for 3V3, black for GND, nothing else in those colours.
   - Crossings are allowed at right angles but count them (`crossings:` line) and
     reduce them by moving parts, not by bending wires around.
4. Render with `--strict`, fix every VIOLATION, then **look**: full PNG, then crops of the
   dense areas at full resolution (headers, module pins, breadboard). Iterate.

## 4. Commands
```sh
PY=reports/2026-10-supervisor/figures/src/.venv/bin/python   # if missing: python3 -m venv <dir>/.venv; <dir>/.venv/bin/pip install cairosvg pyyaml pillow
T=tools/wiring_diagram/wiring_diagram.py
$PY $T SPEC --print-pins nuc '^connector11[5-6]'
$PY $T SPEC --output-dir OUT --grid
$PY $T SPEC --output-dir OUT --strict          # exit 1 on any violation
$PY -m unittest discover -s tools/wiring_diagram/tests
$PY -c "from PIL import Image; Image.open('OUT/x.png').crop((1140,640,1640,900)).save('crop.png')"
```

## 5. Review checklist (PASS only when every line holds in the PNG)
- `--strict` prints `check: PASS`.
- Every wire traceable end to end; no two wires share a segment; no stubs or dead ends.
- Each end on the right hole: Arduino vs morpho, inner vs outer row, top vs bottom header;
  each module wire on the named pin (zoom in; a wrong SVG transform shifts pins by one pitch).
- No text crossed by a wire; titles not overlapped; nothing clipped at the canvas edge.
- Colours match the legend; 3V3 red and GND black only.
- Part credits (Fritzing, CC-BY-SA 3.0) on the figure and in the caption; note what is
  illustrative (placement) versus bench fact (connections) and what is not measured.

## 6. Deliver
Commit spec, parts, outputs. Do not edit a report's `main.tex` while someone else
is editing it; hand over the include line instead, e.g.
`\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/breadboard-wiring.pdf}`.
