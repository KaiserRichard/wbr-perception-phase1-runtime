**Why blocked (KNOWN + INFERRED).**
- The 30 Oct 00:53 summary (fd2013b) records canfix05 *started with GO*, with BOOT0 still fitted. With BOOT0 fitted, the ROM bootloader starts only after a RESET.
- The AGV canfix firmware has no software reset path: no `BOOTLOADER` magic, no `NVIC_SystemReset` (checked on branches f446-home-test and agv-f446).
- The ST-LINK is not on the Pi's USB (`lsusb`), so SWD reset/flash is not possible either.
- The UART was silent at 115200 and 460800 for 3 s each.
- **Only a physical RESET press unblocks this.**

**Why not replay into `/dev/ttyAMA0` now.** The board runs canfix05, the CAN motor home-test firmware. Streaming 460800-baud TerrainAdvisory frames into it would feed unknown bytes to motor firmware (AGENTS.md safety boundary). The replay was therefore run up to a pseudo-terminal served by `tools/link_bench/host/sim.c`, which is the LED image's own `bench.c` logic.
