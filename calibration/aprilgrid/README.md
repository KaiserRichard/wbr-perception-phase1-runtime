# Kalibr AprilGrid target for the D435i calibration (OPEN)

Two print-ready targets, the same 6×4 t36h11 Kalibr grid at two sizes. Each PDF has a matching Kalibr target YAML.
Generated by `make_aprilgrid.py` (no dependencies). The layout reproduces Kalibr's `kalibr_create_target_pdf` at commit
1f60227 (2-bit black border, 180° code rotation, corner squares, IDs 0–23 from the bottom-left tag).

| File | Paper | Tag size (nominal) | Grid extent incl. corner squares | Use at |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `aprilgrid_6x4_45mm_A3.pdf` / `.yaml` | A3 landscape | 45 mm, spacing 0.3 | 364.5 × 247.5 mm | **Preferred.** 0.4–0.8 m |
| `aprilgrid_6x4_30mm_A4.pdf` / `.yaml` | A4 landscape | 30 mm, spacing 0.3 | 243.0 × 166.5 mm | Fallback. 0.3–0.5 m |

CALCULATED with fx = 386.95 px: a 45 mm tag spans 44 / 29 / 22 px at 0.4 / 0.6 / 0.8 m, i.e. 4.4 / 2.9 / 2.2 px per code cell.
A 30 mm tag spans 39 / 23 px at 0.3 / 0.5 m. ASSUMED: keep at least ~2 px per cell, so don't use either target farther away than the table says.

Verification (MEASURED on the Mac, 2026-10-14): both PDFs were rasterized at 10 px/mm with Ghostscript. All 24 tags decode
as t36h11 IDs 0–23 at the expected positions, and the grid width matches the nominal value to 0.1 mm. The 587 extracted
codes have a minimum Hamming distance of 11 under rotation, as 36h11 requires. Detection by Kalibr itself is NOT yet
tested (UNKNOWN until the Kalibr Docker image runs).

This replaces the plan's earlier 6×6 / 80 mm suggestion. That grid is 0.58 m wide and does not fit on A3.

## Print

1. Use a **laser printer** on matte paper. Some inkjet inks are nearly invisible in near-infrared, and the D435i IR cameras
   (emitter off) may then see a blank sheet (INFERRED risk).
2. Print at **100 % / "Actual size"**. Turn off "Fit to page", "Shrink oversized pages" and any scaling. Single page, landscape.
3. Check the **100 mm scale bar** under the grid with a steel ruler. If it is not 100.0 mm (±0.5 mm), fix the print settings and reprint.

## Measure and correct the YAML

The YAML holds the **nominal** `tagSize`. Replace it with the measured value. `tagSpacing` is a ratio, so uniform print
scaling does not change it.

- Horizontal: measure from the **left edge of tag 0 to the left edge of tag 5** (bottom row). Measure from the outer edge
  of the black square, not the corner squares. Nominal: 292.5 mm (A3) / 195.0 mm (A4).
  `tagSize = D / 6.5`. Measure again on the top row.
- Vertical: measure from the **bottom edge of tag 0 to the bottom edge of tag 18**. Nominal: 175.5 mm (A3) / 117.0 mm (A4).
  `tagSize = D / 3.9`.
- Use a steel ruler or caliper read to 0.5 mm. If the horizontal and vertical values differ by more than 0.5 % (ASSUMED), the
  printer stretched one axis. Kalibr takes only one `tagSize`, so reprint on another printer.
- Put the mean in `tagSize` (metres, 4 decimals). Record all readings in the take manifest.

## Mount

- Glue the print **flat to a rigid board**: foam board ≥5 mm, acrylic, MDF or glass. Use spray adhesive or full-coverage
  double-sided tape, and roll out any bubbles. Avoid tape only at the corners, because the paper will bow.
- Check flatness by laying a straightedge across the diagonals. The goal is ≤1 mm gap (ASSUMED).
- Keep a white margin around the grid, and don't cover any corner square with tape or a clamp.
- At the bench, the target stays fixed (wall or table) and the camera moves. Before recording, check that both IR views show
  crisp black and white tags with the emitter off. If they look grey or blank, add daylight or incandescent/halogen light.
  Don't turn the emitter on.
